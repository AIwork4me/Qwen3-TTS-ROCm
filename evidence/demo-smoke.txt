[2] First REST synthesis attempt (pre-fix) caught a real cold-start bug:
  POST /gradio_api/call/generate_custom_voice {"data": ["custom-voice", ...,"Chinese","Vivian","",null x8]}
  -> SSE error: Value: Chinese is not in the list of choices: ['Auto']
  Root cause: dropdowns seed choices=['Auto'] until the model lazily loads;
  gradio 6 rejects out-of-list values. Fixed with allow_custom_value=True on the
  Language/Speaker dropdowns (backend lookup_display maps case-insensitively),
  matching upstream whose post-load build always had full choice lists.
